# Budget and production planning

Estimate human production effort separately from vendor/API spend. Break down concept, script, storyboard, asset creation, editing, motion, 3D, sound, localization, QA, revisions and delivery. Do not claim a market rate or fixed €500 value without local market data and agreed scope.

For third-party generation, capture provider/model, quantity, length, resolution, retries, unit price, currency, tax, credits and cap. If price is not visible in current official docs or account console, mark unverified and request confirmation. Use a low-resolution proof of concept before full production.
