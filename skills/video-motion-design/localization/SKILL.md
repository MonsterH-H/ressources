# Localization and versioning

Treat every language as a separate edit version. Start from the approved transcript, correct proper nouns and terminology, then adapt meaning, tone, idiom and speaking duration. Do not translate technical terms mechanically.

For each language produce an adapted script, localized on-screen copy, SRT/VTT captions, voice track if requested, timing/alignment and QC report. Recompute text wraps and safe areas. Align captions to the final voice track. Ensure captions preserve meaning and do not obscure key visuals.

When original voice must remain unchanged, provide translated subtitles only and state that voice was not dubbed. Do not claim localized audio exists until generated and listened to.
