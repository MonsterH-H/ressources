Use animation to direct attention and explain, not decorate. Build responsive type/layout systems, refined easing, hierarchy, transitions, and coherent scene continuity. Choose GSAP/HTML/SVG for HyperFrames or React/Remotion APIs for Remotion. Never assume browser time equals video time. Seed procedural randomness. Test keyframes at exact times and at phone scale. Avoid unnecessary dependencies and heavy effects on constrained machines.
